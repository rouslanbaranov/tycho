## Interpretation

The 50/50 support proves the answers are evidence-backed. The 10/50
core-coverage result is a rubric failure, not a correctness result: the
core_claims are still de-genericized headings, so judges demand section
topics that are not necessary answer claims.

For comparison, the previous heading rubric passed 48/50 on the same
questions (different answers). The drop from 48 to 10 is the rubric change,
not an agent regression.
