## What this establishes

- the v2 contract can require evidence for every claim and keep citations
  derived from evidence;
- mechanical validity is not semantic validity: 50/50 resolved citations but
  only 23/50 support=2;
- full v3 without a length cap does not fix semantic support by itself;
- judge severity varies sharply, so a single judge score is not reliable.
