# v6 results review — 2026-11-01

Status: AI-authored review of the v6 run; not independent human review and not a
new source of truth. v6 is consumed; do not tune it for a better score.

## Verdict

v6 is a valid exploratory AI-only evaluation: 50 fresh questions, full v3,
no answer-length or claim cap, dual AI judging and adjudication. Its headline
**43/50 is not a stable quality estimate**. The two judges are sharply
asymmetric, the citation metric is inconsistent, and one adjudicated pass was
overridden for a factual error. Treat 43/50 as a consensus under this judge
pair, with a 95% Wilson interval of about **73.8%-93.0%**, not as proof of
production readiness.

- final pass: 43/50;

- support=2: 49/50;
- coverage=2: 44/50;

## Finding 1 — judge asymmetry is the main threat

The two passes do not behave alike:

- judge A (j0): passes 49/50; fails only Q245;
- judge B (j1): passes 43/50; fails 7;
- all six disagreements are A pass / B fail; there are no B-only passes;
- Cohen's kappa is about **0.223**, despite 88% raw agreement.

High raw agreement is inflated by a high pass base rate. The consensus score is
effectively judge B's stricter reading, and the 88% figure should not be
presented as strong reliability.

## Finding 2 — citation_valid is not a clean metric

All v6 citations resolve to indexed sections: **zero bad keys**. But 18 answers
omit at least one precomputed required_evidence anchor. Only Q220, Q222 and
Q232 were marked citation-invalid, and only by judge B. Examples Q205, Q211,
Q213, Q215, Q216, Q218 and Q219 also omit anchors yet were marked valid by both.

The judges are mixing citation resolution, required-anchor coverage and
topical relevance. The 47/50 value is therefore not a trustworthy citation
validity rate; it is a judge-specific blend.

## Finding 3 — Q206 shows coverage-only adjudication can pass a false claim

Q206 says 16->17 modulo retains 1/272 (~0.37%) of keys. The correct retention is
1/17 (~5.9%): keys congruent to 0..15 mod 272 keep the same residue, which is
16/272. Judge A passed Q206; judge B failed it for the arithmetic; the
adjudicator passed it on coverage alone.

The final record overrides Q206 to fail. That is the right outcome, but it
shows the adjudication prompt must include factual support, not only coverage.

## Finding 4 — Q218 contamination was handled correctly

The first Q218 run read part of Q200 to learn the schema. It was quarantined and
a clean run was produced with an explicit schema. The final 50 use the clean
Q218. This is a strength of the process, not a result to hide.

## Finding 5 — full v3 is expensive and changes the regime

v6 answers average 3,478 words and 62 calls; the range is 1,829-8,258 words.
Failed answers are not shorter (mean 3,318 words). This is not comparable with
v5 concise (~210 words, ~30 calls). Any v5/v6 delta confounds prompt mode,
answer length, retrieval budget and question set.

## Layer view

- book: 22/25; Wilson 95% CI about 70.0%-95.8%;
- atlas: 10/12; about 55.2%-95.3%;
- modeling-and-simulation: 11/13; about 57.8%-95.7%.

The atlas and M&S intervals are too wide to support a layer ranking. The earlier
v5 atlas collapse is not reproduced here, but this is a small sample.

## What is supported

- a 50-question fresh full-v3 run can be completed;
- layer-targeted retrieval works for book, atlas and M&S;
- the median answer covers claims at high support under this judge pair;
- contamination can be detected and quarantined;
- the process can produce an auditable adjudication record.

## What is not supported

- that 43/50 generalizes to other judges, models or corpora;
- that v6 beats v5 (different prompt and length regime);
- that citations are 94% valid in a resolution sense;
- that judge agreement is strong (kappa 0.223);
- production validity, human acceptance or owner sign-off.

## Required next actions

1. Report all three numbers side by side: judge A 49/50, judge B 43/50,
   consensus 43/50. Do not headline 43 alone.
2. Rebuild citation scoring: key resolution, required-anchor coverage and
   topical support as separate fields.
3. Calibrate judges on known-good and known-bad answers, including a false
   arithmetic case; require support checks in adjudication.
4. Have a human or different-vendor AI review a stratified 20% sample.
5. Keep Q206 failed; do not let coverage-only adjudication overturn it.
6. Do not compare v6 with v5 until a new corpus is run in a fixed regime.
7. Merge only after the branch's premerge and review gates are resolved.

## Limits

This review used the saved results, judge files, answers and logs. It did not
re-run agents or judges, did not read the underlying book evidence for every
claim, and is not independent human review. No book/atlas content was changed.
