## Editorial decisions

- **Total owner coverage:** 43 new chapters in this ledger + 26 earlier distinct owners = **69/69** with one or more selected claim/evidence entries. This is *coverage of a central statement*, **not** exhaustive chapter verification. The 43 new claims do not replace the 27 earlier checks.
- **Web-source independently opened in this new set:** 34/43. **Citation only / inspect exact text before claim acceptance:** 9/43, especially OpenJDK JEP 491, Reactor 3.7 scheduler reference, Java 25 jcmd, lexical-index lifecycle action, performance modeling and several operational handbooks.
- **Cross-volume consistency:** timeout≠undo, publisher acknowledgment≠DB effect, index relevance≠authorization and diagram/stated SLO≠implemented behavior remain coherent in the sampled central sections. This is not a proof that no contradiction exists anywhere in 136 documents.
- **Open source-version gates:** specific JDK/driver implementation, Redis cache fencers, SQL/Cypher clause behavior, Kafka broker/ISR configuration and SLO denominator should remain conditional when no matching runtime fixture has run.
- **Disposition:** all 69 primary chapters remain `DEEPENED — REVIEW OPEN`, **0 independently accepted**. For closing #213, separately resolve runtime/evidence gaps and conduct full claim-level review, rather than turn these table rows into blanket approvals.
