## Per-chapter publication gate

For **each** chapter: (1) inspect actual prose and supporting links at precise source/version scope; (2) answer the owner question including state authority, normal behavior, partial failure and recovery where relevant; (3) explain alternatives, reversal signals and negative evidence; (4) provide one worked example and text equivalent for material diagrams; (5) resolve canonical ownership and adjacent-chapter duplicate explanations; (6) record evidence class — independently published report, official/spec reference, original hypothetical scenario, unexecuted proposed test, or unknown; (7) record actual review and checks without inventing them. If an engineering concern is out of scope, explain why. A file/heading/URL count is not an acceptance gate.

**Row state:** `OPEN` means substantive investigation remains; `DEEPENED — REVIEW OPEN` records new original explanatory material but **still requires source/semantic acceptance**. The last column is a testable acceptance probe, **not** evidence that the described experiment ran. Published company case claims are never generalized without independent technical grounds.
