## Critical numerical finding: Uber heap improvement cannot be recomputed from cited extrema

The [Uber Cinnamon first-party post](https://www.uber.com/us/en/blog/from-static-rate-limiting-to-intelligent-load-management/) literally reports **~60% lower heap usage**, alongside **1 GiB maximum** and **5–6 GiB spikes**. The simple ratio from just those extrema is approximately 80–83% reduction, **not 60%**. This need not mean the company's metric is false: these likely use different windows/cohorts/aggregation. But the source does not give a comparable denominator in the cited paragraph. The case's existing warning is essential; **do not silently recalculate a 60% fleet mean from peak values or reproduce those percentages as one internally reconciled measurement**. Mark the percentage `publisher-reported, denominator unresolved`.

Other numerically anchored first-party examples were checked against directly retrievable source sentences: Netflix Zuul logging gateway ~25% / ~25% but not API gateway; Airbnb 4.5 years, 38%→68% and 3–5x; Uber DOMA ~2,200; Discord 99.9999% stalled; LinkedIn >800 billion messages/day; Cloudflare migration 15m30s vs 3m25s. All remain self-report and time-scoped.
