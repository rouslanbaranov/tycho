# v7 results review — 2026-11-01

Status: AI-authored review of the completed v7 run. Not human review and not a
new source of truth.

## Verdict

v7 is a successful test of the v2 evidence contract and a failed test of
one-shot semantic grading. The contract is mechanically sound: 50/50 answers
have a claim-to-evidence ledger, all citations resolve, and no orphan
citations remain. The semantic result is weak: **22/50 consensus pass**, only
23/50 support=2, and all 16 judge disagreements fail adjudication.

The 95% Wilson interval for 22/50 is about **31.2%-57.7%**. This is not a
stable quality estimate.

## Counts

- answers: 50/50; judge files: 10/10;
- consensus pass: 22/50;
- judge agreement: 34/50 (68%); Cohen's kappa: **0.351**;
- support=2: 23/50; coverage=2: 28/50;
- citation resolution: **50/50**;
- adjudicated: 16; adjudicator passes: **0**;
- mean answer: 3,522.8 words; mean calls: 79.28.

## What the contract fixed

- every answer has claims with evidence entries;
- citations are derived from evidence;
- required-anchor coverage is computed separately;
- citation resolution is 50/50 with zero unresolved keys;
- the judge output no longer collapses three contracts into one flag.

This is a real improvement over v6's blended citation metric.

## What it did not fix

### 1. Semantic support is still subjective

Judges received the same evidence quotes and produced pass counts from 3/10 to
9/10. Raw agreement 68% hides a kappa of only 0.351. Sixteen tasks disagreed;
all sixteen failed adjudication. The bottleneck is not retrieval or citation
format; it is the decision "does this quote entail this claim".

### 2. Coverage and support are not independent in practice

Several judges marked coverage=2 while semantic support=1: the answer addressed
the claim but the supplied quote did not establish its specific number, branch
or scope. The current scalar scores invite that split to be collapsed.

### 3. Full v3 length did not buy correctness

Mean length is 3,523 words and mean retrieval 79 calls. Failed answers are not
shorter. More text and more evidence entries do not guarantee that a quote
supports its claim.

### 4. Adjudication is a single global verdict

The adjudicator returned one pass/fail per task. That cannot show which core
claim failed or which quote was insufficient. A global verdict is hard to audit
and easy to rationalize.

## Calibration lesson

Known-bad cases show why claim-level grading is required:

- a false arithmetic claim can be surrounded by correct coverage (the v6 Q206
  case: 1/272 versus the correct 1/17);
- a correct-sounding branch can be absent while the main mechanism is covered;
- a quote can mention a topic without establishing the claimed number, unit or
  scope;
- a global "looks complete" judgment can pass all three.

The next rubric must force one verdict per core claim, with the supporting
quote and a one-sentence reason.

## What v7 supports

- the v2 contract can be enforced mechanically at 50/50;
- the benchmark can collect dual judging and adjudication without reusing
  answers;
- the judge-variance problem is measurable and large;
- semantic support, not citation resolution, is the binding constraint.

## What v7 does not support

- production readiness or owner acceptance;
- a stable 44% quality estimate;
- a layer ranking;
- a v6/v7 comparison (different corpus, contract and judges);
- a claim that longer full-v3 answers improve correctness.

## Required next actions

1. Adopt per-claim semantic verdicts; no global pass without a claim matrix.
2. Calibrate judges on known-good, partial and false-support cases before a run.
3. Report resolution, anchor coverage, semantic support and coverage separately.
4. Keep adjudication claim-level, not task-level.
5. Run a new corpus after the rubric is frozen; do not tune v7.
6. Do not merge or publish v7 as a quality result before external review.

## Limits

This review used saved answers, judge files, adjudication outputs and logs. It
did not re-read every source section and is not independent human review. No
book/atlas content was changed.
