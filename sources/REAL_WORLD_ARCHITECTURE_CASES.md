Status: **curated research inputs, not yet accepted case-study chapters**. Initial discovery: 2026-10-19. Public company-owned technical blogs, named engineer talks/transcripts, public project documentation, and first-party engineering reports are preferred. URLs are external references, not permission to redistribute full text, figures, code, or recordings. This register adds **43 linked primary or practitioner-presented resources from 10 organizations**. Every row must be independently re-opened, dated, scoped, and claim-checked by its assigned author before becoming a Tycho source record. In particular, a conference abstract is *talk scope*, not proof that every proposed feature was deployed. Article content is evidence of what authors report, not independent validation of performance.

## Research admission and usage rules

1. First inspect each primary source in full; record publication date, specific section/talk timestamp, author/publisher, access date, reusable-license limits, source status and revisions for public code. External links alone are provisional candidates.
2. Record **legacy baseline → requirements/workload → failure mode → candidate alternatives → chosen change → phased migration → validation/rollback → measured or reported outcome → cost/risks → subsequent evolution**. Mark genuinely unavailable fields `unknown`.
3. Prefer contemporary sources for 'current practice'; do not combine facts from 2016, 2023, and 2026 into a fictitious simultaneous Netflix deployment. Company-reported metrics are not independently measured.
4. Any talk/web source may inspire original prose, original diagrams and synthetic exercises, but do not bulk-download, mirror or transcribe protected materials without rights. Store URL and precise source-section pointers. Admit code only under existing public-source and revision policies.
5. Preserve contradictions and abandoned ideas; **negative/rollback cases** are first-class. Include workload qualifiers, version constraints, throughput-vs-latency trade-offs and insufficient-evidence outcomes.
6. Link each case to canonical Tycho technical material; do not duplicate the relevant explanation. Eventually index cases by problem, failure, constraint, pattern, architecture transition and verified evidence.
7. New case-study authoring is separate from running Tycho's current chapter wave; no unrequested re-enrichment, CI runs or automatic migration of existing chapters.
